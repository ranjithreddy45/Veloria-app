# Role Family Deep Dive: Projects, Design, Legal & Marketing

## Overview

Specialized internal operational and legal roles.

---

## Role Profiles

### 1. PROJECTS_EXEC & PROJECTS_HEAD
- **Exact Code Values**: `"PROJECTS_EXEC"`, `"PROJECTS_HEAD"`
- **Function**: Manage venue renovation projects, construction timelines, and contractor deliverables.

### 2. DESIGN_EXEC & DESIGN_HEAD
- **Exact Code Values**: `"DESIGN_EXEC"`, `"DESIGN_HEAD"`
- **Function**: Produce 3D venue renders, floor plan designs, and event décor schematics.

### 3. LEGAL
- **Exact Code Value**: `"LEGAL"`
- **Function**: Review client contracts, landlord lease agreements, vendor contracts, and regulatory compliance.

### 4. MARKETING
- **Exact Code Value**: `"MARKETING"`
- **Function**: Manage marketing campaigns, promotional offers, social media integration, and lead generation ads.
