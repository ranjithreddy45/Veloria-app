# 07 - Vendor Categories & Classification

---

## 🏷️ Enum Classifications (`VendorCategory`)

| Enum Value | Category Name | Typical Scope |
|---|---|---|
| `CATERING` | Food & Catering | External food preparation, live counters, service staff |
| `DECORATION` | Stage & Mandap Decor | Floral arrangements, drapery, stage fabrication |
| `PHOTOGRAPHY` | Photography | Event photo capture, portrait sessions |
| `VIDEOGRAPHY` | Videography & Live Stream | Video recording, drone capture, live streaming |
| `ENTERTAINMENT` | DJ, Band & Performers | Music, artists, choreography, MC services |
| `FLORIST` | Floral Supplies | Fresh flower supply and centerpieces |
| `TRANSPORTATION` | Guest Transport | Buses, luxury cars, valet coordination |
| `LIGHTING` | Architectural Lighting | Ambient lights, spot lights, intelligent fixtures |
| `SOUND` | Public Address & Sound | PA systems, microphones, mixers, speakers |
| `SECURITY` | Event Security | Bouncers, door guards, crowd management |
| `CLEANING` | Post-Event Housekeeping | Waste management, hall cleaning, sanitation |
| `RENTAL` | Furniture & AV Rentals | Chairs, tables, cutlery, LED screens |
| `OTHER` | Miscellaneous | General specialty vendors |
