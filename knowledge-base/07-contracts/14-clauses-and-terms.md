# Contract Clauses & Standard Terms

## Overview

Standard venue policies, cancellation schedules, catering rules, and liability clauses embedded in contract templates.

---

## Standard Clause Sections

1. **Payment & Advance Schedule**: Deposit percentages, interim payments, final settlement deadlines.
2. **Cancellation & Refund Policy**: Escalating penalty percentages based on cancellation date.
3. **Venue Rules & Music Hours**: Noise restriction cutoffs, liquor license compliance, and damage security deposits.
