# Contracts & Agreements Business Purpose

## Overview

The Contracts module protects venue operations and client commitments through legally binding digital agreements, automated template generation, digital signature enforcement, and audit-ready document archives.

---

## Core Business Objectives

1. **Legal Binding Compliance**: Formalize terms, cancellation policies, venue rules, and payment schedules prior to event execution.
2. **Standardized Legal Templates**: Ensure consistency across all client agreements using admin-controlled contract templates and dynamic variable substitution.
3. **Frictionless Remote E-Signing**: Enable clients to view, review, and digitally sign contracts on desktop or mobile devices via tokenized public URLs.
4. **Audit Trail & Lock Integrity**: Freeze and lock contract text (`isLocked = true`) upon signing, recording cryptographic signature data, signer IP address, and user-agent metadata.
