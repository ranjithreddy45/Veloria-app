## Notification Dispatch (`notifyClaimStep`)

- **Submission Alert**: Sent to Level 1 approvers via in-app `Notification` and Resend email.
- **Approval Escalation**: Sent to Level 2 approvers upon Level 1 sign-off.
- **Final Decision Alert**: Sent to employee upon claim approval, rejection, or `NEEDS_INFO` request.
