# 21 Employee Self-Service Capabilities

`CODE VERIFIED`

## ESS Console (`/me/reimbursements`)

Employees can:
1. Submit new claims with category, amount, fuel liters, and bill uploads.
2. Track real-time approval status (`PENDING`, `PENDING_L2`, `APPROVED`, `PAID`).
3. View 2-level approval history and decision notes.
4. Withdraw pending claims before final approval (`cancelMyReimbursement`).
