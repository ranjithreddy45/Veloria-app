# Public Routes (23 Endpoints)

## Overview

Public routes (`(public)` / `/events`, `/venues`, `/careers`, etc.) are fully unauthenticated marketing and informational pages accessible to website visitors.

---

## Public Route Index

- **`/`**: Veloria Grand homepage and venue showcase.
- **`/venues`**: Venue catalogue, hall capacities, and photo galleries.
- **`/q/[quoteToken]`**: Public quotation viewer & client acceptance portal.
- **`/sign/[contractToken]`**: Public contract e-signature page.
- **`/careers`**: Job openings and career application portal.
- **`/contact`**: Contact and inquiry submission form.
