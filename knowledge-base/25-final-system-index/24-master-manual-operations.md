# Phase 24: Master Manual Operations

## Human Operational Requirements
1. **Attendance Finalization**: HR Executives must manually inspect biometric sync exceptions and approve manual log adjustments before payroll runs.
2. **Payroll Disbursal Approval**: HR Director and Finance Director must review and sign off payroll runs prior to bank disbursement.
3. **Government Statutory Filing**: GST returns, Provident Fund (PF), and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) filings require external portal submissions.
4. **Physical Inventory Audit**: Periodic physical stock checks are required to log stock variance adjustments in the kitchen module.
5. **On-Site Event Operations**: Physical BEO execution, food tasting approval, and venue setup require human coordinators.
