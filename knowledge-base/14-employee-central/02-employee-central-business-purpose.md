# 02 Employee Central Business Purpose & Objectives

## Core Business Objectives

1. **Centralized Identity & Profile Management**: Maintains comprehensive staff profiles, contact information, emergency contacts, and education records.
2. **Organizational Hierarchy**: Maps departmental structures and reporting relationships to drive multi-tier approval flows for Leave, Expenses, and Approvals.
3. **Statutory & Financial Compliance**: Captures PF, ESI, PAN, Aadhaar, and Bank Account details necessary for statutory compliance and automated salary disbursements.
4. **Lifecycle Management**: Governs employee onboarding, probation, status transitions (`ACTIVE`, `ONBOARDING`, `ON_LEAVE`, `EXITED`, `SUSPENDED`), and offboarding.
