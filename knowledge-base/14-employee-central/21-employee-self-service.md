# 21 Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal

## ESS Features (`src/app/(dashboard)/people/`)

- Profile view (`/people/[id]`).
- My Payslips (`/people/my/payslips`).
- Leave Requests & Balance (`/people/leave`).
- Reimbursement Submission (`/people/payroll/reimbursements`).
- Attendance Punching & Muster (`/people/attendance`).
