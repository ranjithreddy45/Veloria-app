# 18 Employee Statutory Information

## Statutory Details (`EmployeeStatutory`)

- `panNumber`: Indian Permanent Account Number.
- `aadhaarNumber`: Indian UIDAI Aadhaar ID.
- `uanNumber`: Universal Account Number (EPF).
- `pfNumber`: Provident Fund registration.
- `esiNumber`: Employee State Insurance ID.
- Managed via `src/actions/hr-statutory.actions.ts`.
