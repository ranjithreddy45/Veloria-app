# 22 Revenue Accounting & Recognition

## Principle of Revenue Recognition

Revenue is recognized at the moment of **Invoice Issuance** (`postInvoiceIssued`).

---

## Revenue Categories & COA Mapping

- **4010 - Event Sales Revenue**: Main venue booking packages, catering, decor, and add-on services.
- **Tax Exclusion**: GST (CGST/SGST/IGST) is excluded from revenue and credited to tax liability accounts (`2210`, `2220`, `2230`).
