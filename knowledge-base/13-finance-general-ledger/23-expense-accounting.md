# 23 Expense Accounting

## Operational Cost Centers (`5010`-`5230`)

The system categorizes operational expenses across dedicated GL expense codes:

- `5010`: Food & Beverage Expense
- `5020`: Venue & Infrastructure Expense
- `5030`: Decor & Floral Expense
- `5040`: Audio/Visual & Lighting Expense
- `5050`: Logistics & Transport Expense
- `5060`: Staffing & Security Expense
- `5070`: Licenses & Permits Expense
- `5080`: Miscellaneous Event Expense
- `5230`: General Supplies Expense
