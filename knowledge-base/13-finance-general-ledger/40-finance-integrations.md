# 40 External & Internal Finance Integrations

## Integration Endpoints

- **Razorpay Payment Gateway**: Webhooks & order APIs for automated AR settlement.
- **Tally ERP / Tally Prime**: XML export engine (`tally-xml.ts`).
- **GSTN E-Invoicing**: IRN adapter (`einvoice-adapter.ts`).
- **Resend / WhatsApp**: Automated payment receipts and invoice delivery.
