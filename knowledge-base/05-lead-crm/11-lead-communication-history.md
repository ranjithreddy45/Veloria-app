# Lead Communication History

## Overview

Tracks multi-channel communication touchpoints with clients across Phone, WhatsApp, Email, and In-Person Visits.

---

## Communication Touch Attributes

- **`Lead.touchCount`**: Total count of outbound/inbound touches.
- **`Lead.lastTouchedAt`**: Timestamp of latest communication.
- **`Lead.lastTouchKind`**: Enum / string (`NOTE`, `CALL`, `EMAIL`, `SMS`, `WHATSAPP`, `MEETING`).
- **`Lead.lastTouchedById`**: User ID of staff member who initiated contact.
