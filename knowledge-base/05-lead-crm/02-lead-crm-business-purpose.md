# Lead CRM Business Purpose & Scope

## Overview

The Lead CRM module serves as the primary revenue engine for Veloria Grand banquet halls and luxury venues. It captures inquiries from all digital and offline channels, ensures rapid response times through SLA tracking, automates sales team distribution, and streamlines lead qualification to maximize booking conversion rates.

---

## Core Business Objectives

1. **Zero Lead Leakage**: Capture 100% of incoming inquiries across website forms, Meta Lead Ads, Google Ads, IndiaMART, JustDial, WedMeGood, WhatsApp, and phone calls.
2. **Speed-to-Lead Optimization**: Enforce strict SLA clocks (e.g., 5-minute first response deadline) to maximize response rates and win rates.
3. **Automated Team Distribution**: Eliminate manual allocation delays through intelligent round-robin routing based on rep availability and workload.
4. **Intelligent Qualification & AI Scoring**: Automatically score leads by event size, budget per plate, and purchase intent to prioritize high-value leads.
