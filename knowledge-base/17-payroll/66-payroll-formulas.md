# 66 Critical Payroll Formula Register

## Verified Formula Register

1. **Basic**: `Monthly CTC * (basicPct / 100)`
2. **Special Allowance**: `Monthly CTC - Sum(Basic + Fixed Earnings)`
3. **Pay Factor**: `(30 - lopDays) / 30`
4. **Paid Gross**: `Sum(Monthly Earnings * Pay Factor) + Arrears + Reimbursements`
5. **Employee PF**: `12% of Min(Basic, 15000)`
6. **Employee ESI**: `0.75% of Gross` (if Gross <= 21000)
7. **Net Pay**: `Gross - Total Deductions - Advance Recovery`
