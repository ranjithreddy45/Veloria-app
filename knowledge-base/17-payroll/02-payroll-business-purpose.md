# 02 Payroll Business Purpose & Objectives

## Core Business Objectives

1. **Accurate Salary Calculation**: Computes monthly gross, statutory deductions, LOP proration, and net salary using standardized pure math.
2. **Statutory Compliance**: Enforces Indian statutory rules (FY 2026-27 New Tax Regime, EPF 12% split, ESIC 0.75%/3.25%, Professional Tax slabs, TDS projections).
3. **Double-Entry Financial Integration**: Automates balanced GL journal postings into the General Ledger upon payroll finalization.
4. **Employee Self-Service**: Renders downloadable PDF payslips and salary breakdown views for venue staff.
