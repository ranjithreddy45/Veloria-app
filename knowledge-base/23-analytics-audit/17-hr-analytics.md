## Headcount & Attrition Analytics (`src/actions/hr-analytics.actions.ts`)

- **Active Headcount**: Total active employees grouped by Department, Designation, and Employment Type.
- **Monthly Attrition Rate**: `(Number of Exits in Month / Average Active Headcount) * 100`.
- **Gender & Diversity Metrics**: Demographics distribution report across departments.
- **Tenure Analysis**: Average employee tenure by department.
