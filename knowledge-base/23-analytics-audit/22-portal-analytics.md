## External Stakeholder Activity Metrics

- **Quotation Link Views (`QuoteView`)**: Tracks view count, client IP, and time spent on shared quotation links (`/q/[token]`).
- **Digital Signature Speed**: Time elapsed between `SignatureRequest` issuance and client signature confirmation.
- **Vendor Bid Response Rate**: Ratio of RFP invitations answered by vendors in portal (`/vendor-portal/bids`).
- **Guest RSVP Conversion**: Percentage of invited guests responding via PWA (`/app` or `/rsvp/[token]`).
