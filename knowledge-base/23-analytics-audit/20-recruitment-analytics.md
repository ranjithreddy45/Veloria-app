## Talent Acquisition Analytics (`src/actions/recruit.actions.ts`)

- **Candidate Funnel**: Stage progression (`APPLIED` -> `SCREENED` -> `INTERVIEWED` -> `OFFERED` -> `HIRED`).
- **Time to Fill**: Days from `RecJobOpening.createdAt` to candidate offer acceptance date.
- **Offer Acceptance Rate**: `(Accepted Offers / Total Offers Issued) * 100`.
- **Channel Sourcing Efficiency**: Cost and volume comparison across job portals and agency referrals.
