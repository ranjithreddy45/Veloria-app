## Payroll Analytics Engine (`src/actions/hr-report-salary.actions.ts` & `hr-statutory-reports.actions.ts`)

- **Gross vs Net Payroll**: Total salary payout breakdown across earnings, allowances, and deductions.
- **Statutory PF Report**: Monthly Provident Fund return statement (`12% Employee + 12% Employer`).
- **Statutory ESI Report**: Employees State Insurance return statement (`0.75% Employee + 3.25% Employer`).
- **Professional Tax (PT) & TDS**: State-wise PT slab breakdown and Tax Deducted at Source summaries.
