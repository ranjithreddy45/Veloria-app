# Quotation Notifications & Staff Alerts

## Overview

In-app notifications and email alerts triggered during quotation state changes.

---

## Notification Triggers

- **Approval Required**: Alert sent to `SALES_HEAD` when quote status reaches `PENDING_APPROVAL`.
- **Quote Viewed**: Real-time push alert to Sales Exec when client opens proposal link (`QuoteView`).
- **Quote Accepted / Paid**: Instant notification to Sales Exec, Finance, and Ops when deposit is paid.
