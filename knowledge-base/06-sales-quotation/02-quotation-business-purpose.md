# Quotation Business Purpose & Objectives

## Overview

The Sales Quotation module empowers sales teams to create accurate, multi-tiered event proposals, enforce margin controls via manager discount approvals, maximize yield using date-demand multipliers, and accelerate deal velocity through tokenized public sharing and one-tap booking deposits.

---

## Core Business Objectives

1. **Standardized Pricing & Margins**: Eliminate pricing errors through automated per-plate calculations, itemized catalog pricing, and standard GST tax slabs.
2. **Dynamic Yield Optimization**: Automatically adjust pricing for high-demand dates, peak seasons, weekend slots, and short lead times using configurable yield rules.
3. **Multi-Tiered Proposal Variants**: Present Silver, Gold, and Platinum tier packages on a single public proposal page to increase average contract value (ACV).
4. **Instant Client Acceptance & Payment**: Enable clients to view interactive proposals on mobile, track dwell time via Quote Radar, and secure dates instantly via one-tap deposit payment.
