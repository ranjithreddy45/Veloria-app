# Service Integrations

## Overview

External services integrated with the quotation engine.

---

## Integrations

1. **Razorpay Checkout**: Powers one-tap deposit payment on public proposals (`/q/[token]`).
2. **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API**: Delivers tokenized proposal links and venue catalogs directly to clients.
3. **Resend Email API**: Sends HTML proposal emails with embedded PDF download links.
4. **Puppeteer / PDF Generator**: Generates server-side PDF proposals (`/api/quotations/[id]/pdf`).
