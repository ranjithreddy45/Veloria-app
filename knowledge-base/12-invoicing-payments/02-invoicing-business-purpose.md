# 02 - Invoicing Business Purpose

---

## 🎯 Commercial & Financial Objectives

The Invoicing & Payments module serves five essential commercial purposes:
1. **Financial Contract Enforcement**: Transforming sales quotations (`Quote`) and event bookings (`Booking`) into legally binding Tax Invoices.
2. **Tax Compliance**: Complying with Indian GST regulations by computing CGST (9%), SGST (9%), or IGST (18%) based on Place of Supply.
3. **Cash Flow & Milestone Management**: Structuring payment schedules into milestone installments (`Installment`) with automated due-date notifications.
4. **Digital Payment Friction Reduction**: Providing instant online payment links (`/pay/[token]`), QR codes, and multi-payer split links (`/pay/split/[token]`) via Razorpay.
5. **Auditable Accounting & GL Integration**: Ensuring real-time synchronization between billing transactions and the double-entry General Ledger (`FinJournalEntry`).
