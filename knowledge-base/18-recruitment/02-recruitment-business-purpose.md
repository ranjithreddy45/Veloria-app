## Strategic Goals
1. **Talent Acquisition**: Streamline hiring across hotel departments (Food & Beverage, Housekeeping, Front Desk, Kitchen, Sales, Executive Management).
2. **Candidate Experience**: Provide clean, responsive public career pages (`/careers`) with built-in DPDP consent logging (`recordConsent`).
3. **Auditability & Compliance**: Track candidate background checks (`RecBgvCheck`) and offer letter history with strict state machine transition validation.
4. **Seamless HR Handoff**: Transform hired candidates into `Employee` records in single click via `createEmployeeFromCandidate()`.
