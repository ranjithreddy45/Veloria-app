# 34 - Estimated vs Actual Food Cost Reconciliation

---

## 📊 Food Cost Variance Analysis

- **Estimated Food Cost (`estFoodCost`)**: Computed upon kitchen plan creation from planned ingredient quantities and estimated unit rates.
- **Actual Food Cost (`actualFoodCost`)**: Computed post-cooking after the Chef enters `actualUnitCost` for prep items.
- **Cost Variance %**:
  ```
  Variance % = ((actualFoodCost - estFoodCost) / estFoodCost) * 100
  ```
- **Gross Margin Impact**: Cost variances directly impact banquet gross margin reports.
