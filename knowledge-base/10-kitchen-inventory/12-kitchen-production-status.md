# 12 - Kitchen Production Status & Lifecycle

---

## 🔄 Production Stages

1. **`DRAFT` / `PLANNED`**: Initial prep plan created; quantities estimated.
2. **`IN_PROGRESS`**: Active batch prep in progress in the main kitchen.
3. **`COMPLETED`**: Dishes cooked, hot-held, and handed over to banquet service stewards. Satisfies Optional Gate 4 in `computeOperationReadiness()`.
