## 🍳 Event-Day Kitchen Workflow

1. **Morning Prep**: Kitchen team reviews BEO function sheet and `KitchenPlan` covers.
2. **Batch Cooking**: Chef moves plan status to `IN_PROGRESS`.
3. **Plating & Handover**: Prepared food is plated and staged for banquet stewards. Chef moves plan status to `COMPLETED`.
