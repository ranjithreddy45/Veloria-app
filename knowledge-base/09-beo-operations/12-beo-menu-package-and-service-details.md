## 🍴 Catering & Service Specifications

- **Catering Style**: Buffet, plated sit-down dinner, live food stations, or cocktail pass-arounds.
- **Menu Items (`BookingMenu`)**: Categorized breakdown of starters, main courses, breads, rice dishes, live counters, desserts, and beverages.
- **Dietary & Specialty Badges**: Clear tagging for Jain items, pure veg counters, gluten-free options, and nut allergy disclaimers.
- **Bar & Liquor Service**: License details, corkage terms, bartender headcount, and bar closure times.
