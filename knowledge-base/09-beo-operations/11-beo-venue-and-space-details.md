# 11 - BEO Venue & Space Specifications

---

## 🏛️ Venue Setup Parameters

The BEO specifies physical setup requirements for banquet halls and outdoor lawns:
- **Hall Section (`Booking.hallBooked`)**: Specific hall partition or lawn zone (e.g. "Grand Ballroom - North Wing").
- **Seating & Table Arrangement**: Cluster, theater, classroom, round-table, or U-shape setup linked via `SeatingChart`.
- **Audio/Visual & Lighting**: Stage dimensions, LED wall specs, cordless mics, ambient wash lighting specs.
- **Air Conditioning & Power Backup**: AC runtime schedules and DG set generator standby requirements.
