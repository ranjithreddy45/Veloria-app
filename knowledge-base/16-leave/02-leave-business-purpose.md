# 02 Leave Subsystem Business Purpose & Objectives

## Core Business Objectives

1. **Entitlement Governance**: Enforces annual leave limits per leave type (CL, SL, EL, Comp-Off).
2. **Workflow Automation**: Routes leave applications to designated managers (`Employee.reportingManagerId`) or HR admins.
3. **Attendance & Payroll Integrity**: Distinguishes paid leave (which protects salary) from unpaid leave (which generates Loss of Pay `lopDays`).
4. **Calendar Visibility**: Renders team and company-wide leave calendars to prevent operational staffing shortages during event executions.
