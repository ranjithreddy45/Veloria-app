# Phase 01: Borders, Radii & Shadows

## 1. Border & Radius Standards
- **Global Radius**: `--radius: 0.625rem` (10px).
- **Card Border Radius**: `rounded-xl` (12px) or `rounded-lg` (8px).
- **Button / Input Radius**: `rounded-md` (6px).
- **Badge Radius**: `rounded-full` (Pill shape).
- **Borders**: 1px solid structural border (`border border-border`).

## 2. Shadow & Glassmorphism Surfaces
- **Glassmorphism Chrome**: `@utility bg-material-sidebar` utilizing `backdrop-blur-md` with frosted fill.
- **Card Shadow**: `shadow-sm` or `shadow-md` for elevated overlays.
