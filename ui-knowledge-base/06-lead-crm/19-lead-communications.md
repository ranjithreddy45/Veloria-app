# LEAD COMMUNICATIONS UI FORENSICS

## 1. Communication Channels

1. **WhatsApp Console Integration**: Direct click-to-chat button opening `/whatsapp/console?phone=...`.
2. **Click-to-Dial Phone**: Initiates mobile dialer or CRM call log popup (`CallDispositionDialog`).
3. **Email Engagement Card**: `ContactEngagementCard` tracking email opens and link clicks.
